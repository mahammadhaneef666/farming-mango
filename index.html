Our Story

Haneef Mango Farm is a family-owned agricultural enterprise based in Nandyalampeta, Andhra Pradesh. For generations, we've been cultivating the finest mango varieties on our 3-acre farm.

We believe in pure, organic farming practices and direct customer relationships. No middlemen, no compromises on quality.

Every mango from our farm is a promise of freshness and taste.
